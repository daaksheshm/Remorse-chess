Psychological Warfare — Complete Rule Book & Probabilities

1. Post-Capture Trauma Rolls (1d6 Probabilities)

Every capture forces a trauma roll:

  • Roll 1 (16.7%): Guilt-Ridden (Immobilized for 3 turns, self-eliminates if not rescued).
  • Rolls 2–3 (33.3%): Pacifist / Devastated (Cannot make captures).
  • Rolls 4–5 (33.3%): Normal composure maintained.
  • Roll 6 (16.7%): Bloodlusted (Optional bonus move/capture unlocked immediately).

2. Debuff Kill Counter Reset

Whenever a piece receives any negative debuff or trauma (Guilt or Pacifist), its accumulated kill counter immediately resets to 0.

3. Bishop Self-Cure (1.0 Valor)

If a Bishop becomes Devastated, it can self-cure for 1.0 Valor as a free action and still move normally.

4. Psychotic Threshold (3 Kills)

Reaching 3 kills turns a piece Psychotic. It must make a capture to move, and can target friendly pieces (except the King).

5. Absolute King Protection

The King can never be captured directly by any piece (friend or foe).
